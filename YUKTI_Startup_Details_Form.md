### Long Form Answers (Strictly <= 100 Words Each)

#### 1. Problem – Solution Fit (88 words)
Higher education institutions face severe academic record fragmentation and widespread attendance fraud. Manual roll calls consume 10-15% of instructional time, while proxy check-ins plague existing QR, GPS, and Bluetooth systems due to screenshot sharing and signal leakage outside classrooms. Furthermore, poor campus connectivity causes cloud-only attendance apps to fail. EduVault solves this for universities, faculty, and students by combining a centralised academic ERP with an offline-first ultrasonic attendance engine. Wall-confined acoustic signals ensure genuine physical presence, while automated sync ensures instant compliance tracking and early student intervention.

#### 2. Product-Market Fit (87 words)
EduVault is positioned at TRL 4 (laboratory-validated system) and MRL 3 (bench-level pilot readiness). The software stack (Spring Boot, React, PostgreSQL) operates in a functional staging environment, delivering centralized grading, question archives, and academic tracking. The physical attendance pipeline has validated acoustic beacon transmission (18–21 kHz) and Android Keystore hardware-backed cryptographic signing on prototype hardware. To satisfy institutional reliability demands, the solution operates offline-first, eliminating expensive biometric hardware bottlenecks and campus network dependencies. This validated architecture directly meets colleges' need for an affordable, fraud-proof attendance system.

#### 3. Market Size: TAM, SAM, SOM (91 words)
EduVault targets higher education institutions, engineering colleges, and universities across India. The Total Addressable Market (TAM) covers ~45,000 higher education institutions across India valued at over INR 3,500 Crores annually in EdTech and campus ERP spending. The Serviceable Available Market (SAM) comprises ~10,000 AICTE/UGC accredited technical and engineering institutes spending on attendance automation, accreditation compliance, and LMS. The Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) focuses initially on 150 regional engineering and polytechnic colleges in Western India (Maharashtra/Karnataka) representing an estimated initial ARR of INR 4.5 Crores via annual SaaS licensing.

#### 4. Business Fit & Traction (92 words)
EduVault operates on a B2B SaaS subscription model with tiered annual licensing per student (INR 150-300/student/year) alongside low-cost classroom beacon deployments. Current commercialization readiness is at early validation: a functional prototype was piloted within the institute department across 120+ students and 4 faculty members for academic tracking and attendance simulations. The pilot validated high user adoption, zero recurring hardware maintenance costs, and reduced attendance overhead. Go-to-market commercialization leverages institutional network referrals, AICTE IIC showcases, and modular adoption starting with attendance before expanding into full academic vault management.

#### 5. Competitive Advantage / USP (91 words)
EduVault's primary USP is tamper-proof physical proximity verification using room-confined ultrasound, eliminating proxy attendance without costly biometric kiosks. Competitive advantages include: (1) Ultrasonic containment—18-21 kHz signals cannot penetrate walls, stopping corridor check-ins; (2) Hardware-backed cryptographic signing—Android Keystore StrongBox keys prevent app cloning or virtual device spoofing; (3) Offline resilience—operates seamlessly without live classroom Wi-Fi via local encrypted nonce caching and batch synchronization; and (4) High ROI—leverages students' existing smartphones and inexpensive ESP32 beacons (<$5/room), lowering deployment costs by 80% compared to traditional biometric attendance hardware.
